Siri AI is rolling out to macOS 27, even in the EU - despite this kind of Apple Intelligence not coming to European iPhones or iPads, at least not initially. Along with the Siri AI system, macOS exhibits direct access to AFM3 Core - the LLM at the foundation.

## AFM3 command line client

macOS 27 comes with two primary ways to interact with this new type of Apple Intelligence: the new Siri app (more below) and the `fm` command line tool. This requires accepting the terms & conditions first:

> % fm
>
> YOU HAVE NOT AGREED TO THE APPLE FOUNDATION MODELS CLI LEGAL NOTICE & TERMS.
>
> Agreeing to the Apple Foundation Models CLI Legal Notice & Terms applies to every user on the machine, so it must be run as a privileged user (e.g. ‘sudo fm license’).
