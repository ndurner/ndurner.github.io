[OpenAI Daybreak](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001258-openai-daybreak-trusted-access-for-cyber-overview), the program that allows certain models to be used for cyber security work, requires hardening of consumer accounts since October 1. The measures required include Advanced Account Security to be enabled. Moreover, OpenAI require user to “add at least one supported physical FIDO2 credential, such as YubiKey or Google Titan”. This is vague as to what model to actually buy, and the partnered promotional bundle consists of a “YubiKey C NFC” and a “YubiKey C Nano” - for 71.40€. What’s worse, I could not find a way to conclusively confirm whether a particular enrollment actually secures continued access to Daybreak.
