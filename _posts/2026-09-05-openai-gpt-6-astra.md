GPT-6 has rolled out to the API and several ChatGPT subscription types (including the EU), and coming to the rest really soon.

(I have updated [MyChatty](https://github.com/ndurner/mychatty) to support GPT-6 as well, including Pro mode)

The [System Card is here](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-6-astra/gpt-6-astra.pdf), with extensive notes on the model’s safety/robustness/alignment properties. OpenAI report improvements in resistance against prompt injection attacks:

Some benchmarks, as well as user sentiment on X, place Astra at or above Anthropic’s Fable 5.1 level. Most notably, GPT-6 achieves 99.9% accuracy on the [ARC-AGI-3 benchmark](https://arcprize.org/blog/astra). (ARC-AGI-3 is a benchmark for studying agentic intelligence through novel, abstract, turn-based environments.). While GPT-6 scored the same as GPT-5.6 on the Artificial Analysis index, this amalgamation has been changed meanwhile to [form version 4.2](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index-v4-2) - and GPT-6 now [ranks between Opus 5 and Fable 5.1](https://artificialanalysis.ai/#artificial-analysis-intelligence-index).
